My Story

I'm Renz Angelo M. Tabios, also known as Koro Artz. I'm a fourth-year Information Technology student and a creative who loves video editing, 2D animation, digital and traditional art, and songwriting.

I make videos for TikTok and YouTube, mixing smooth cuts, fun motion, and storytelling. I also take art and animation commissions from students and other clients, and I enjoy bringing ideas and imagination to life frame by frame.

Alongside my creative work, I study Web Systems Technology and completed an online Virtual Assistant course, so I can combine creativity with technical and organizational skills.
